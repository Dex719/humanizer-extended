Great question! Here's a look at why our team switched to weekly releases.

Shipping weekly isn't just a process change — it's a cultural shift. When we moved from monthly to weekly releases in March, the goal wasn't speed. It was predictability.

The results speak for themselves. Rollbacks dropped from 9 in the first quarter to 2 in the second, and the average pull request now waits 1.5 days for review instead of 4. Faster feedback. Smaller diffs. Fewer surprises.

To be clear, this isn't about working harder. It's about working in smaller steps — a pivotal shift that underscores our commitment to quality, fostering trust across the team.

And that's the real win.
